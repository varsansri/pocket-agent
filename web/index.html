How it works

  1. Say what you want. Type it, or tap the microphone and speak.
  2. Watch it work. The AI opens websites, reads them, taps buttons and fills in boxes on your screen, step by step.
  3. Say yes or no. Before anything that sends, pays, books or deletes, it stops and asks you. Then it tells you the result, out loud if you like.

What you can ask it

  • Compare prices for something on a few shopping sites.
  • Look up train, bus or flight times.
  • Fill in a long form using details you asked it to remember.
  • Explain a confusing page in simple words.
  • Find an answer on a website so you don't have to scroll.
  • Play a turn-based web game like 2048 or tic-tac-toe while you watch.

Made for people who just want it done

  • No setup and no technical words.
  • Type or just say what you want. It answers out loud.
  • Nothing is sent, paid or deleted without your yes.
  • It never types your passwords or OTPs. You stay in control.
  • Stuck? Message us from inside the app and a real person replies.

Questions

Is Hanubees free?

Yes. The app is free. It uses Google's Gemini AI through your own free Gemini key, which takes about a minute to get at aistudio.google.com. The app shows you how.

Which phones does it work on?

Android phones with Android 8.0 or newer. There is no iPhone version yet.

Can it pay or send things without me?

No. Before anything that sends, pays, buys, books, posts or deletes, it asks you Yes or No on the screen. It never types passwords, OTPs, PINs or card numbers. You type those yourself.

Does "Sign in with Google" work inside it?

Not yet. Google blocks that button inside apps like this, so use a site's email or phone-number login instead.

What happens to my data?

Details you ask it to remember, and your Gemini key, stay on your phone. Your requests go to Google Gemini to be answered. To make the app better we record how it is used, including what you ask the AI and your approximate city. We never sell it. Read the privacy page.
