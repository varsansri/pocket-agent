Privacy

Last updated 27 November 2026.

Stays on your phone

  • Things you ask it to remember ("Saved info"), such as an address.
  • Your Gemini key.
  • Your browsing history and website logins (cookies), like any browser.

Hanubees never saves passwords, PINs, OTPs or card numbers, and never types them for you.

Sent to Google Gemini

When you give the AI a task, your words and the text of the page it is working on (and sometimes a screenshot) go to Google's Gemini AI, using your own key, so it can decide what to do. Google's terms for the Gemini API apply.

What we record

  • Your account: name, email, and mobile number if you give it.
  • How the app is used: when it opens, time spent, the tasks you give the AI (your words, the result, the websites it visited), Yes/No answers, and errors.
  • Your phone: model, Android version, app version and language.
  • Approximate place: country and city, worked out from your internet address. We do not store the address itself.
  • Messages you send us from "Talk to us".

We use this only to run and improve Hanubees and to reply to you. We never sell it. It is stored with Supabase and Vercel, our hosting providers.

Deleting your data

Open the app, tap ☰ → Talk to us, and ask us to delete your account. We will remove your account and messages.
